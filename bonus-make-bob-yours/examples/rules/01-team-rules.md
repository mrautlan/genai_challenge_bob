# Team rules

- Before a bigger change (more than one file or more than ~50 lines), show me a short plan and wait for my OK.
- After every change, explain in at most 3 bullet points what you changed and why, in simple words.
- Add a short comment above every function you write.
- Never add external libraries, frameworks or CDN links unless I explicitly ask for them.
- If something is unclear, ask me instead of guessing.
